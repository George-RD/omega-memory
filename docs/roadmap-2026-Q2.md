# OMEGA Roadmap — Q2 2026

## ICP
Senior financial engineers and quant developers using AI coding assistants (Claude Code, Cursor) on complex multi-repo trading/fintech systems. Teams of 2-10 with budget authority to expense $50-500/mo dev tooling. Local-first is a requirement, not a preference.

## Vision
Every AI coding agent remembers what matters — decisions, preferences, context — so financial engineering teams never start from scratch.

## Strategic Goals
1. **Become the memory layer for AI-assisted financial engineering teams** — paying customers in finance, MRR (Now)
2. **Ship self-serve Pro tier with payment flow** — MRR, free-to-paid conversion (Now)
3. **Build finance-specific proof points and content** — case studies, benchmarks, inbound search (Next)
4. **Expand to adjacent high-value verticals** — healthtech, infra/platform (Later)
5. **Build community flywheel around sovereign AI memory** — stars, contributors, organic mentions (Later)

## Decisions & Trade-offs
- **Finance-first, not everyone**: Narrowing to finance means saying no to generic developer marketing. A page that says "for financial engineering teams" will convert 10x better than "for developers."
- **Revenue before community**: Community is the long game but paying customers fund everything. Pro tier + payment flow is Now, community programs are Later.
- **Local-first as feature, not limitation**: In finance, data sovereignty is a buying criterion. Position OMEGA's local-first architecture as a competitive advantage over cloud-dependent alternatives (Mem0, Zep).
- **Build features when customers ask**: Multi-repo memory and team sharing scored low on RICE despite being real features. Defer until paying customers validate demand.
- **Outreach before content marketing**: At <100 stars, inbound won't work. Direct outreach to finance teams is the fastest path to first customers. Content supports outreach, not the other way around.
