### RECITALS

A. The Assignor is the original author and sole copyright holder of the software known as "OMEGA" (also referred to as "omega-memory"), a persistent memory system for AI coding agents, including all source code, documentation, tests, configuration files, and associated materials (collectively, the "Works").

B. The Stichting was established for the purpose of investing in and promoting technology, and wishes to acquire the copyright in the Works to steward, maintain, and license them as an open source project.

C. The Assignor wishes to assign the copyright in the Works to the Stichting to ensure the long-term stewardship and availability of the Works.

---

### AGREEMENT

In consideration of the mutual promises herein and for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

#### 1. Assignment of Copyright

1.1. The Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Assignee all right, title, and interest in and to the copyright in the Works, including but not limited to:

   (a) All source code of the OMEGA software as it exists on the date of this Deed, including all files in the repository located at the Assignor's development environment;

   (b) All documentation, including but not limited to README files, guides, API reference documentation, architectural documentation, and internal documentation;

   (c) All test suites, configuration files, build scripts, and deployment configurations;

   (d) All artwork, logos, and design assets created for the OMEGA project;

   (e) The right to reproduce, distribute, publicly display, publicly perform, create derivative works, and sublicense the Works;

   (f) The right to register the copyright in any jurisdiction;

   (g) All rights to enforce the copyright against infringers; and

   (h) All other rights of whatsoever nature in and to the Works, whether now known or hereafter recognized, in all languages, formats, and media, throughout the world, for the full term of such rights.

1.2. This assignment includes all versions, revisions, and modifications of the Works created by the Assignor prior to the date of this Deed.

#### 2. Future Works

2.1. The Assignor agrees that all original works of authorship created by the Assignor for the OMEGA project after the date of this Deed shall be automatically assigned to the Assignee upon creation, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

2.2. To the extent that automatic assignment is not effective under applicable law, the Assignor hereby grants the Assignee an irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, exclusive license to the future works described in Section 2.1, with the right to sublicense.

#### 3. Moral Rights

3.1. To the extent permitted by Dutch law (Auteurswet, Articles 25 and 25a), the Assignor waives the exercise of moral rights (persoonlijkheidsrechten) in the Works against the Assignee and its sublicensees, including the right to object to modifications of the Works.

3.2. The Assignor acknowledges that the Works may be modified, adapted, or incorporated into larger works by the Assignee or its licensees, and consents to such modifications.

3.3. The Assignor retains the right to be identified as the original author of the Works where attribution is given.

#### 4. Representations and Warranties

The Assignor represents and warrants that:

4.1. The Assignor is the sole author and copyright holder of the Works;

4.2. The Works are original and do not infringe upon the intellectual property rights of any third party;

4.3. The Assignor has not previously assigned, licensed exclusively, or encumbered the copyright in the Works in any way that would conflict with this Deed;

4.4. There are no pending or threatened claims against the Assignor relating to the Works; and

4.5. The Assignor has full authority to enter into this Deed and perform the obligations hereunder.

#### 5. Further Assurances

The Assignor agrees to execute any additional documents and take any further actions that may be reasonably necessary to effectuate and confirm the assignment of copyright contemplated by this Deed.

#### 6. Consideration

This assignment is made for good and valuable consideration, including the Stichting's commitment to:

(a) Maintain and steward the Works as an open source project under the Apache License, Version 2.0;

(b) Ensure the continued availability and development of the Works for the benefit of the public; and

(c) Apply grant funding and other resources to further develop and improve the Works.

#### 7. Governing Law

This Deed shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands. Any disputes arising under this Deed shall be submitted to the competent court in Almelo, The Netherlands.

#### 8. Entire Agreement

This Deed constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous communications, representations, or agreements, whether oral or written.
